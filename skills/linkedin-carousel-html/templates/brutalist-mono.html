{{PUBLICATION_NAME}}Mistake 01 of 05

Two ideas fighting on one slide

A reader on a phone sees one slide at a time. When that slide carries two ideas, neither one lands and the next swipe feels like work.

Give every idea its own slide, even if the deck gets longer.

Read your densest slide out loud. If you say "and also", split it in two.
