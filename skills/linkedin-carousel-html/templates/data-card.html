Mistake 01 of 05 · density
Before
2

ideas fighting on one slide, and neither lands.

After
1

idea per slide, and the reader keeps up.

Give every idea its own slide.

A reader on a phone sees one slide at a time. When that slide carries two ideas, the next swipe feels like work. Read your densest slide out loud. If you say “and also”, split it in two.

Mistake 02 of 05 · legibility
1080px

wide on your canvas, a few inches wide on a phone.

You build each slide at 1080 by 1350 pixels, usually on a large monitor. The reader meets it on a phone, where the whole slide is only a few inches across.

Text that felt comfortable on your screen shrinks with it. Check every slide at phone size before you publish.
