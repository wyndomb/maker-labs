01Mistake 01 of 05 · Density

Two ideas fighting on one slide

A reader on a phone sees one slide at a time.

When that slide carries two ideas, neither one lands and the next swipe feels like work.

Give every idea its own slide, even if the deck gets longer. One clear point per slide is easier to finish.

Try todayRead your densest slide out loud. If you say "and also", split it in two.
