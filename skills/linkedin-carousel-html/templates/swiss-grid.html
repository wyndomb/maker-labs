Mistake 01

A reader on a phone sees one slide at a time. Two ideas on it compete.

01

Two ideas
fight on
one slide.

When a slide carries two ideas, neither one lands. Give every idea its own slide, even if the deck gets longer.

Read your densest slide out loud. If you say "and also", split it in two.
