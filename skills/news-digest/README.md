# News Digest

Get a concise briefing on a topic, organization, or several related subjects. The default response appears in chat and covers the past 48 hours. State a different period, region, or audience when it matters.

Try:

- "Give me the past 48 hours of AI and robotics news."
- "Summarize this week's renewable energy policy news in Indonesia for a small business owner."
- "What changed in photography gear this week?"
- "Turn that digest into a visual HTML briefing."

Each story explains what changed, when it happened, why it matters, and where the information came from. Quiet periods can produce shorter digests. Sources and research availability determine what can be verified; the digest does not promise exhaustive coverage.

Live news needs available search and source-reading tools. No Tavily installation or special connector is required. Without live access, the skill can summarize supplied material and clearly label that limitation. Optional HTML needs file creation for a downloadable artifact; otherwise complete HTML source can be provided.

The former AI News Digest skill is now News Digest. Requests for AI news continue to work with AI as the topic. Historical development logs are not part of this reader package.
