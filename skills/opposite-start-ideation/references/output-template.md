# Output Template

Use this structure for the final brief. Keep it concise and centered on differentiation.

```markdown
## Opposite Start Brief: [Topic]

**Audience**: [audience]
**Research window**: [dates or timeframe]
**Research mode**: [three delegated scouts or single-agent fallback]
**Search note**: [Search tool used and material coverage limits]

### Crowded Narrative Map

| Cluster | Status | Repeated claim | Underlying assumption | Why it is crowded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [name] | [crowded or emerging] | [claim] | [assumption] | [independent recurrence across source types] |

### Open Space

- [Ignored consequence, missing audience, category error, power shift, time delay, or contradiction]
- [Second open space]
- [Third open space]

### Differentiated Angles

| Candidate angle | Closest crowded take | What substantively changes | Distance | Relevance | Collision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [angle] | [nearest common argument] | [mechanism, consequence, protagonist, category, time, power, or conclusion] | [1-5] | [1-5] | [low or medium] |

### Recommended Angle

**Angle**: [one sharp sentence]

**Why this has the most open space**: [Explain the narrative distance, audience relevance, and collision result in 2 to 4 sentences.]

**Closest existing take**: [Summarize and link the nearest result.]

**The distinction to preserve**: [State the boundary that keeps this angle from collapsing back into the crowded narrative.]

### Optional Hooks

1. [hook]
2. [hook]
3. [hook]

### Compact Source Note

- [Representative sources with links and dates]
- [Coverage or access limits]
```

Return 3 to 5 candidates when the evidence supports them; return fewer if needed. For a source-only provisional brief, mark collision as unassessed and clearly state that live collision checks were not performed. Do not include forced angles, high-collision candidates, proof plans, story prompts, closing lines, archive checks, author-evidence checks, tier decisions, or a finished draft unless the user asks for them.
