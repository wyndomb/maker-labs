# Plan Patterns

Use the pattern closest to the user's task. Fill the HTML template with the relevant sections. Do not include every possible detail if it makes the plan harder to review.

## 1. Newsletter Or Paid Post Planning

Use for newsletter ideas, paid-post outlines, title choices, paywall planning, and draft setup.

Template: `assets/content-plan-template.html`

For requests without a subscription or paid-content model, omit tier and paywall sections.

Include:

1. Reader problem.
2. Why this is worth publishing.
3. Free versus paid boundary.
4. Proposed opening.
5. Main sections.
6. Evidence, examples, or source material needed.
7. Paid deliverable, such as template, workflow, checklist, files, prompts, or walkthrough.
8. Voice and quality checks.

Good human decisions:

1. Is this free, paid, or paired free plus paid?
2. Is the reader payoff concrete enough?
3. What implementation detail should stay behind the paywall?

## 2. Research Project Planning

Use before web research, competitive scans, source analysis, market research, or tool comparisons.

Template: `assets/research-plan-template.html`

Include:

1. Research question.
2. What would change the recommendation.
3. Search paths.
4. Source types to trust.
5. Source types to avoid.
6. Comparison criteria.
7. Output format.
8. Confidence and caveat rules.

Good human decisions:

1. What is the real decision this research should support?
2. What sources are mandatory?
3. What claims need current verification?

## 3. Agent, Automation, Or Workflow Build

Use before building an agent, skill, command, automation, file workflow, or technical setup.

Include:

1. Job the system should do.
2. Inputs.
3. Outputs.
4. Folder or file structure.
5. Tools or connectors.
6. Human approval points.
7. Failure cases.
8. Tests or checks.
9. Smallest useful version.

Good human decisions:

1. Should this be manual, semi-automated, or automated?
2. What should the agent never do without approval?
3. What does a passing test look like?

## 4. Content Repurposing

Use when turning one source into LinkedIn posts, Substack Notes, X threads, carousels, emails, or short-form content.

Template: `assets/content-plan-template.html`

Include:

1. Source material.
2. Core idea.
3. Platform-specific angles.
4. What each platform should not repeat.
5. Voice constraints.
6. Output count.
7. Review criteria.

Good human decisions:

1. Which angle is strongest?
2. Which platforms should mention the original tool or source?
3. What should stay out because it sounds generic?

## 5. Dashboard, Spreadsheet, Or Data Visualization

Use before building dashboards, spreadsheets, CSV summaries, charts, or data tools.

Include:

1. Data inputs.
2. Questions the dashboard should answer.
3. First-screen priority.
4. Chart choices.
5. Filters or controls.
6. Raw data versus visual summary split.
7. Data quality risks.
8. Validation checks.

Good human decisions:

1. What is the first question the dashboard must answer?
2. What data should remain raw?
3. What chart would mislead the reader?

## 6. Client, Team, Or Stakeholder Approval

Use when another person needs to review AI-assisted work before execution.

Include:

1. Plain-language summary.
2. What AI will do.
3. What the human still reviews.
4. Approval points.
5. Timeline.
6. Scope boundaries.
7. Risks.
8. Final deliverables.

Good human decisions:

1. Who needs to approve what?
2. What is out of scope?
3. What should be shown visually before work begins?

## 7. Decision Support

Use for tool choices, strategy choices, product decisions, content direction, or prioritization.

Template: `assets/decision-board-template.html`

Include:

1. Decision to make.
2. Options.
3. Criteria.
4. Cost or effort.
5. Risk.
6. Reversibility.
7. Recommendation.
8. Next action.

Good human decisions:

1. What criterion matters most?
2. What option is easiest to reverse?
3. What would make the recommendation wrong?
