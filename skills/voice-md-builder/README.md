# Voice Builder

Build one portable `VOICE.md` that captures how you write long-form and how you speak, so any AI tool or editor can write in your voice.

## What you need

- At least three long-form pieces you wrote yourself, ideally four or more and about 3,000 words in total: newsletters, essays, articles, or long posts. Pasting the text or uploading files works best. Links often come back shortened or stop at a paywall, so Voice Builder checks and asks for the full text when that happens. You can also point to documents in Google Drive or Notion.
- A mix of the kinds of writing you do. A profile built only from tutorials will not know how you write a personal essay.
- Optional spoken material. Pick any of these, or none: Wispr Flow dictations and meetings, Granola transcripts, Zoom transcripts, Google Drive transcripts (such as Google Meet), or transcripts you paste or upload. Only your own words are used; other people in a meeting are never quoted or stored.
- About 15 minutes for a short interview and a review of the result.

## What you get

A single `VOICE.md` with an instruction to the reader at the top, your voice in five lines, your written voice, your spoken voice and how it should carry into writing, Always and Never rules with quoted evidence, before-and-after examples, a Corrections section, and an honest Sources and confidence section.

Before you see the final file, Voice Builder writes a test passage using only the file and compares it with one of your real pieces. What feels off becomes a correction.

## Where it lives

Wherever you like. The file works on its own, with or without this plugin: keep it in a folder, Notion, Google Drive, or a Claude Project, and attach it when you write. In Cowork or Claude Code it can be saved straight to a folder you choose. In Claude chat you get the file to download. Plugin updates never touch it.

## Using it

Attach or paste the file and say: "Write this using my VOICE.md as the source of truth."

## Improving it

Come back with new samples or a correction, for example "I'd never say 'here's the thing'", and ask Voice Builder to update your voice file. It revises the file and keeps your earlier corrections.

## Try it

"Use Voice Builder to create my VOICE.md. Here are three of my newsletter issues, and use my Wispr Flow dictations for my spoken voice."
